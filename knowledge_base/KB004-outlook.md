# Outlook Email Synchronisation

## Symptoms
Outlook is not synchronising email or appears to be offline.

## Troubleshooting
1. Confirm that the computer has a working internet connection.
2. Check whether Outlook is in Work Offline mode.
3. Restart Outlook and wait for synchronisation.
4. Check for service notifications from the organisation.
5. If the problem continues, record the error and contact IT support.

## Security
Do not delete mail profiles or data files without authorisation.
