# VPN Connectivity Troubleshooting

## Symptoms
The employee cannot connect to the corporate VPN or authentication repeatedly fails.

## Troubleshooting
1. Confirm that the internet connection works.
2. Verify that the employee is using the approved VPN client and server.
3. Check that the system date and time are correct.
4. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN.
5. Restart the approved VPN client and retry.
6. If the problem continues, record the error message and escalate to Network Support.

## Security
Never request or store the employee's password.
